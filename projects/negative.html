NEGATIVE IMPACT OF TECHNOLOGY IN WATCHMAKING

Negative Impact:

The advancements of technology in the watch industry has brought significant chnages, but it also poses negative impacts. Traditional craftmanship is degrading as huge production and automation replace the need for skilled artists. They also threate centuries-old techniques and heritages. The increase of different smartwatches has brought consumer partiality, leading to decline in mechanical watch sales, while also contributing to electronic waste due to their short lifespan and limited repairability. Counterfeiting has become more advanced with the use of 3D printing and digital tools, damaginng the exclusivity and reputation of luxury watch brands.

Additionally, due to over-reliance on mechanization. It has led to the jobless in traditional watchmaking industries, reducing chances for talented workers. Many modern watches, particularly digital and quartz models, lack the emotional and artistic value of handcrafted timepieces, making them more disposable and less sentimental for collectors. Furthermore, planned obsolescence in smartwatches and some modern quartz watches results in shorter lifespans. Because they are often difficult or expensive to repair compared to traditinal mechanical watches. While technology has enhanced precision and efficiency, it has also led to environmental concerns, with non-biodegradable materials and increasing e-watse. Overall while advancement has made watches more accessible and functional, they also have diminished craftmanship, sustainability, and long-term value that define traditional horology.
