Final Analysis

Visual Design


The overall aesthetic of website reflects the Rado-like aesthetic in the LUX distinction with professional quality photos and consistently dark blue color scheme. But it does not have the basic design as Rado. Overall, our websire appeared to be even more luxurious compared to Rolex, even our logo is better then Rado and Rolex.

User Interface


The interface that our website has provided is simple and easy to use like the Rolex watches. The thematic doesn’t fit Rado in a case of the UI; it is more youthful and modern compared to Rado which still has a functional UI, but it doesn’t have any features that make it pop.Overeall, Our website is better.

User Experience


The major concept of user interface experience of our website is guided by a vision of simplicity and smoothness.But Bishopwatches is more smooth; however, Rolex makes it quite exciting and fashionable. Rado is more functional than esthetic and doesn’t have the sleek look of the real thing.

Technologies Used


While Bishopwatches and our website focus on superior looks and probably lightweight customize solutions, the Rolex and Rado blaze the horn for ‘innovative touch technologies,’ placing a high value on interactive features.

Navigation Structure


Just like Bishopwatches, Our website provides the easy and initial clear dropdown menus. Rolex has lots of subcategories that make more sense to a wider range of customer, while Titan may be appropriately detailed and useful, but often look messy

Features and Functionality


Since our website aims at elegance and not functionality, filters or reviews or other such options may be missing. Rolex maintains all extra features in check for it to feel as luxury as possible and on the other hand Rado has incorporated few more advanced customer related options such as reviews and wishlists.
