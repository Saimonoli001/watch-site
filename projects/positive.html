POSITIVE IMPACT OF TECHNOLOGY IN WATCHMAKING

Positive Impact:

Technology has evolved the watch industry, bringing numerous positive impacts that have transformed how people preceive and use watches. Materials like silicon reduces friction ,improving long life and accuracy. Automation and 3D printing have made manufacture faster and more compatible, allowing watch makers to experiment with new designs. Smartwatches have introduced a whole new way to connect with timepieces, combining classic design with features like health tracking and different notifications.At the same time, watches have become more durable, with better water and shock resistance for active lifestyle. Eco-friendly innovations, such assolar-powered movements and kinetic energy systems, reduce waste and promote sustainability. Digital design tools also allow forgreater customization, letting people personalize their watches to suit their style. Meanwhile, technology has helped preserve traditional watchmaking, making it easier to restore vintage timepieces. Digital archives andN AI-assisted design tools are helping brands push creative boundaries while respecting traditonal techniques. Digtal tools have modernized some aspects but also they accompany traditional handcrafting skills,blending the best of the both worlds.

Technology has revolunized watchmaking, blending traditional craftmenship with cutting-edge advancements. Whether it's making watches more accurate, durable, smart or sustainable, innovation continues to push the boundaries of timekeeping.
